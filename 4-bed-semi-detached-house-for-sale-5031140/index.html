Description

Discover contemporary living in the heart of Crossmolina with this exceptional A-rated home, ideally positioned within a newly constructed development just a short stroll from the town centre. Finished to an impressive standard throughout, this beautifully designed property combines timeless Georgian-inspired architecture, elegant sash windows and quality craftsmanship. Featuring underfloor heating, triple-glazed windows and an air-to-water heat pump, the home offers year-round comfort while keeping running costs to a minimum.

Accommodation comprises 4 bedrooms (1 ensuite), bathroom, WC, sitting room, utility room, and kitchen/dining area.

Outside, the property benefits from private parking, a small green area to the front and a private rear garden, providing the perfect balance of outdoor space and low-maintenance living.

Crossmolina is one of North Mayo's most attractive and welcoming towns, renowned for its strong sense of community and spectacular natural surroundings. Nestled between the shores of Lough Conn and the majestic Nephin Mountain range, the area is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts. Lough Conn is internationally recognised for its excellent fishing, while the nearby Nephin Beg Mountain Range and Ballycroy National Park offer some of Ireland's finest hiking and wildlife experiences. The famous Great Western Greenway is also within easy reach, providing kilometres of scenic cycling and walking routes through the beautiful Mayo countryside.

Everyday convenience is right on your doorstep, with Crossmolina offering an excellent range of amenities including supermarkets, cafés, restaurants, pubs, pharmacies, sporting facilities and healthcare services. Families will appreciate the property's proximity to highly regarded local schools including St. Tiernan's College and Gortnor Abbey National School, all within easy reach.

The larger towns of Ballina and Castlebar are easily accessible, while Ireland West Airport Knock is less than a 1 hour drive away, making this an ideal location for commuters, families and those seeking a relaxed lifestyle without compromising on convenience.

This superb home presents a rare opportunity to enjoy the very best of contemporary living in one of the county's most desirable residential settings.
